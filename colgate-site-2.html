Questions

How do I use my coupon?

Scan the QR code on the brochure, open your coupon, and show it to the cashier when you pay.

Can I use my code more than once?

No. Each code works for one purchase only. After it is redeemed, it is marked as used.

Why does every QR have a different code?

So two people can't use the same coupon. Each QR on the brochure is matched to its own code.

Does the coupon work on a twin pack?

No. The offer is for 1 box (1 toothpaste) only.

Is this an official Colgate offer?

No. This is a student project made for school. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Colgate-Palmolive.
